Who We Are

Global Scrap
Metal
Trading

Double Click Trading FZE operates from Dubai, connecting scrap metal suppliers and buyers across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East since 2020. We specialise in the procurement, processing, and export of ferrous and non-ferrous scrap materials — serving industrial clients who demand consistency, compliance, and competitive pricing.

Established in the Emirate of Dubai as a Free Zone Entity, we leverage the UAE's strategic trade infrastructure to move material efficiently across global markets — with full documentation, quality verification, and transparent chain-of-custody on every shipment.

Commodities

What We Trade

⚙️
Aluminum

UBC, extrusion, cast, and mixed aluminum scrap. Consistent grade separation and clean preparation for smelters across Asia and the Middle East.

🔶
Copper

Bare bright, #1, #2, and insulated copper wire. High-purity batches sourced from certified dismantlers with full provenance documentation.

🏗️
Steel

Structural steel, plate, and beam scrap. Heavy melt grades available in bulk for steel mills — container and bulk vessel shipments.

🔩
Brass

Yellow brass, red brass, and cartridge brass. Verified alloy composition with full analytical reports on every parcel.

⚓
HMS

Heavy Melting Scrap 1 & 2. Reliably graded to ISRI specifications. Available for container-loaded or bulk vessel shipments to major import markets.

♻️
Non-Ferrous Mix

Zorba, Zurik, and mixed non-ferrous streams. Tailored sorting and blending to meet individual buyer specifications and downstream process requirements.
